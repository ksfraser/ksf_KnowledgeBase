# Sun Life ETF+ Portfolios — Fund Family Reference

**Brand:** Sun Life Global Investments &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Manager:** SLGI Asset Management Inc.
**Effective:** 2025-12-08 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Fund-code prefix:** SUN &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Portfolio Manager:** Anthony Wu, CFA

A suite of five actively managed mutual-fund trusts (also available in Sun Life's **segregated fund**
line-up). They blend core mutual funds with passive ETFs (Vanguard, iShares, State Street) and add
targeted allocations to **gold**, **private fixed income** (SLC Management Private Fixed Income Plus Fund),
and a proprietary **U.S. sector rotation** strategy.

> **Rebrand note:** Effective 2025-12-08 the *Sun Life Tactical ETF Portfolios* were renamed the
> *Sun Life ETF+ Portfolios*, adopted exposure to physical commodities, and had their management fees reduced.
